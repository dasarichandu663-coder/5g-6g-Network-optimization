How QuantumNet Works

QuantumNet translates complex 5G/6G wireless network resource allocation into a Quadratic Unconstrained Binary Optimization (QUBO) problem solvable on quantum co-processors.

NETWORK STATE (UE Traffic, CSI, Path Loss, Interference) ↓ RESOURCE ENCODING (Binary Decision Variables for Bandwidth, Power & Beams) ↓ HAMILTONIAN FORMULATION (H_Cost = Capacity Max + Interference Penalty) ↓ QAOA HYBRID SOLVER (Variational Quantum Circuit Optimization) ↓ DECODING & SANITY CHECK (SLA & QoS Validation) ↓ REAL-TIME NETWORK DEPLOYMENT (Dynamic Beam Steering & Power Balancing)

This interactive simulator implements statevector simulations of the QAOA variational loop directly in the browser, showcasing how quantum optimization outperforms classical heuristics.
